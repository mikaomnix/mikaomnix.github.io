Delete your account and data

You can request permanent deletion of your Mekanix account and all associated data at any time.

How to request

  • In the app: open the menu → “🗑️ حذف الحساب” (Delete account) → “Send delete request”.
  • By email: write to apdoma773mod@gmail.com from your account email with the subject “Delete account” and your shop name.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, password) and all staff accounts.
  • All data: products and stock, invoices, customers and suppliers, expenses and settings.
  • Deletion is completed within 7 days; backups are purged automatically within 30 days at most.
  • We may retain only the minimum data required by law, if any.
