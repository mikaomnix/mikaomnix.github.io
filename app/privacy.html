2. إزاي بنستخدم البيانات

  • تشغيل التطبيق وعرض بياناتك ليك ولموظفينك حسب الصلاحيات اللي انت بتحددها.
  • إدارة الحساب والفترة التجريبية والاشتراك، والدعم الفني لما تطلبه.
  • مش بنبيع بياناتك، ومش بنستخدمها في إعلانات، ومفيش أي إعلانات أو تتبّع في التطبيق.

3. مشاركة البيانات

مش بنشارك بياناتك مع أي طرف تالت لأغراضه الخاصة. بنستخدم مزوّدي خدمة بيعالجوا البيانات نيابة عننا وبس:

  • Supabase: تخزين البيانات وتسجيل الدخول (خوادم سحابية مؤمّنة).
  • Google Gemini: قراية صور فواتير الموردين، لو استخدمت الخاصية دي بس.
  • WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta): استقبال رسايل الطلبات، لو انت ربطت رقم واتساب محلك بنفسك بس.

ممكن نكشف بيانات لو القانون أو جهة رسمية مختصة طلبت ده.

4. الأمان

  • كل الاتصالات مشفّرة (HTTPS / TLS)، والبيانات متخزنة مشفّرة على الخوادم.
  • بيانات كل محل منفصلة تماماً على مستوى قاعدة البيانات، ومحل تاني مايقدرش يشوفها.
  • الموظف مابيشوفش أسعار الشراء ولا الأرباح، والحماية دي معمولة على السيرفر.

5. الاحتفاظ بالبيانات

بنحتفظ ببياناتك طول ما حسابك موجود. لو الاشتراك خلص، البيانات بتفضل محفوظة عشان تقدر تقراها لحد ما تطلب مسحها. النسخ الاحتياطية اليومية بتتمسح لوحدها بعد 30 يوم.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Mika Omnix (package com.mikaomnix.app) is an inventory, sales and invoicing app for shops and small businesses. This policy explains what data we collect, why, who processes it, and how to delete it. It applies to the Google Play app and to mikaomnix.github.io.

1. Data we collect

TypeDataPurpose
Account infoEmail, password (hashed, never visible to anyone), shop name, owner name, phone numberAccount creation, sign-in, account communication
Business dataProducts, prices, stock, invoices, customers and suppliers (entered by the shop owner), expenses, staffProviding the app's features only
Supplier invoice photos (optional)Photo of an invoice when you use the scan featureExtracting line items; the photo is deleted after use
Contacts (optional)A contacts file (vcf/csv) that you choose to uploadAdding customers/suppliers after your review. The app never reads the device address book by itself
TechnicalLast sign-in date, subscription statusManaging trial and subscription

We do not collect location, advertising IDs, payment card data, or data from other apps.

2. How we use data

  • To operate the app and show your data to you and your staff according to the permissions you set.
  • To manage your account, trial and subscription, and provide support on request.
  • We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and the app contains no ads or third-party tracking.

3. Data sharing

We do not share your data with third parties for their own purposes. We use these service providers (processors) only to run the service:

  • Supabase — database hosting and authentication.
  • Google Gemini — reading supplier invoice photos, only if you use that feature.
  • WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta) — receiving order messages, only if you connect your own shop number.

We may disclose data if required by law.

4. Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.
  • Each shop's data is isolated at the database level (row-level security).
  • Staff accounts cannot see purchase costs or profits; this is enforced server-side.

5. Retention

We keep your data while your account exists. If a subscription ends, data stays readable until you request deletion. Daily backups are automatically deleted after 30 days.

6. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In the app: Menu → “Delete account”.
  • By email to apdoma773mod@gmail.com from your account email, subject “Delete account”.
  • Web request page: https://mikaomnix.github.io/delete-account.html

We complete deletion within 7 days: the account and all data (products, invoices, customers, staff) are permanently deleted. Backups are purged within 30 days at most. We may retain only the minimum required by law.

7. Your rights

You can view and edit your data in the app at any time, and request a copy or deletion.

8. Children

The app is intended for business owners aged 18+. It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect children's data.

9. Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes and notify you in the app for significant changes.
